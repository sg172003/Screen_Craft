Help

Use ScreenCraft to add a frame, background, and canvas styling to a screenshot or screen recording.

Supported media

  • Images: PNG, JPG/JPEG, and WebP up to 25 MB and 8192 pixels per side.
  • Videos: MP4, WebM, and MOV up to 150 MB and 10 minutes long.

Video playback and export depend on the codecs and recording features supported by your browser. ScreenCraft shows available video formats in the export dialog.

Create an image or video export

  1. Choose a file, drag it into the editor, or paste an image with Ctrl+V on Windows/Linux or Cmd+V on Mac while ScreenCraft is open.
  2. Choose a canvas size or match it to your media, then adjust the frame, background, padding, corners, and shadow.
  3. Export a PNG at 1×, 2×, or 3× the canvas scale. For video, open the export dialog and choose a format supported by your browser.

Your original media file is not changed. The preview may scale it proportionally to fit the selected canvas. The exported image uses the canvas dimensions multiplied by the selected export scale.

Privacy

Media is processed in your browser and is not uploaded by ScreenCraft. Optional Google Analytics can record feature-use events after you allow it; it does not receive media or filenames.
