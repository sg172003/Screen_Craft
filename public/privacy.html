Privacy

Last updated October 16, 2026

ScreenCraft styles screenshots and screen recordings in your browser. This page explains what stays on your device and what optional analytics can send.

Your media stays on your device

When you choose or paste an image or video, ScreenCraft reads and processes it locally in your browser. The app does not upload your media, filename, or clipboard contents to ScreenCraft servers. Exported files are created in your browser and downloaded to your device when you choose to export.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is off unless you choose “Allow analytics.” If you allow it, ScreenCraft sends app usage events to Google Analytics so the developer can understand which features are used. You can change this choice using “Analytics settings” in the landing page footer.

ScreenCraft's event payloads can include the page path and title; whether an upload is an image or video, its size in kilobytes and its dimensions; image export scale and format; video export format and duration; selected frame type and preset name; and whether the install prompt was shown, accepted, or dismissed. These events do not include media bytes, filenames, or clipboard contents. Google Analytics may also process browser, device, and usage information according to the settings of the Analytics property and Google's privacy terms.

After permission is granted, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser storage for measurement. Retention of analytics data is controlled by the Analytics property settings. Read Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's handling of data.

Choosing “Continue without analytics” prevents the Analytics script from loading. If you later withdraw permission, ScreenCraft stops sending events and updates the Google consent state for analytics storage.

Information stored in your browser

ScreenCraft uses browser storage to remember your theme choice (light or dark), install-prompt preference (permanently dismissed or snoozed until a date), and analytics choice (granted or denied). The app's service worker caches the app shell and static files so the site can reopen offline. It does not cache your uploaded media as part of this feature.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the ScreenCraft developer's GitHub profile. Please do not post personal information or private media in public issues.
